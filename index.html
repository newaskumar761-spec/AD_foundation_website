About AD Foundation

AD Foundation is an educational initiative dedicated to providing free learning opportunities for children. Our goal is to make education accessible, practical and enjoyable for every child.


Along with regular subjects, children can learn computer skills, artificial intelligence, graphic design and participate in creative educational activities.
